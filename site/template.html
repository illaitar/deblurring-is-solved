  • Every released checkpoint of a method runs on every test set: video methods on the four video sets, image methods also on RealBlur-J and RealBlur-R, which hold single images. Trained on picks the checkpoints shown: GoPro (the default), the training split of the current test set, any set, or one training set.
  • On RealBlur, PSNR and SSIM follow the dataset's official evaluation: each output is aligned to the sharp image by an ECC homography and scored on the valid region (scikit-image 0.17.2, OpenCV 4.2, as the script requires). LPIPS and the other metrics use the unaligned output.
  • PSNR and SSIM are computed per frame on 8-bit RGB outputs against the sharp frame and averaged over all frames of the set. LPIPS uses the AlexNet backbone.
  • A score is final when at least 95 % of the frames of the set are scored. Runs below that are shown under unfinished runs.
  • Each row is one variant of a method: the whole frame at once, tiles, test-time local converter (TLC), or the temporal scheme of a video method.
  • Compute (GMAC) and latency are measured per output frame at 1280×720, batch 1, on one A100. A method that needs a window of frames to emit one frame is charged for the whole window.
  • Frame characteristics come from the sharp frame and its blurry input: blur as the PSNR of the input and the share of gradient left after blur, texture, brightness, contrast, clipped highlights, input noise (Immerkaer) and colorfulness. Motion on the four video sets is the optical flow between consecutive sharp frames (MEMFOF). Every frame with its characteristics and every method's score on it is in results/frames/bench.sqlite.
